About this page

Career swims come from each season’s team Top 10 lists. A swim that did not make its season’s Top 10 is not in our files, so a missing time is a gap in our records, not in a swimmer’s career.

State places, relay lineups and team finishes come from the official meet results for 2017, 2018, 2019 and 2020. 2018: The official file lists only the finals of this division: a swimmer who swam prelims only is not in it. 2018: The official file gives no relay swimmers this year, so relays are not credited to anyone. 2019: The official file lists only the finals of this division: a swimmer who swam prelims only is not in it. 2020: Swum as a virtual meet (timed finals at each school) because of COVID-19; the file gives no grades.

This class’s list of seniors is taken from swimmers listed as seniors on that season’s Top 10 lists; a senior who never made a Top 10 list is missing from this page. Corrections are welcome.
