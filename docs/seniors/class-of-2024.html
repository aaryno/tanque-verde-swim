About this page

Career swims come from each season’s team Top 10 lists. A swim that did not make its season’s Top 10 is not in our files, so a missing time is a gap in our records, not in a swimmer’s career.

State places, relay lineups and team finishes come from the official meet results for 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023. 2020: Swum as a virtual meet (timed finals at each school) because of COVID-19; the file gives no grades.

This class’s list of seniors is taken from swimmers listed as seniors on that season’s Top 10 lists or in that fall’s official State results; a senior who made neither is missing from this page. Corrections are welcome.
