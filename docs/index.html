🏆 School Records: the Olsson brothers

First Kent, then Wade

At the Canyon del Oro Classic on Sep 19, 2026, sophomore Kent Olsson swam the 500 Freestyle in 5:03.13 and broke two Tanque Verde records in the same swim.

The open school record he took down — 5:04.10, set by Joseph Breinholt on Oct 24, 2015 — was the oldest record on the Tanque Verde boys books. It had stood longer than any other boys school record, individual or relay. The next-oldest still standing is the 50 Freestyle — 21.99, Nicholas Cusson, Oct 23, 2021.

Four weeks later, at the Arena High School Classic on Oct 17, 2026, his older brother Wade Olsson, a senior, swam 4:53.96 — 9.17 seconds under Kent's mark. He also lowered the 200 Individual Medley record to 1:55.22 — beating his own record from the 2025 D-3 AIA State Championship by 2.56s.
