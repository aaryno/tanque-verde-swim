AIA Division III State Championship

Fri–Sat, Nov 6–7, 2026 · Dady Aquatic Center, Phoenix Country Day School, Paradise Valley

‹ Southern Arizona Regional Qualifier
The storyTop swimsFull results
📖 The story

Wade Olsson wins two state titles, and the boys 200 medley relay wins in school-record time

Senior Wade Olsson is a state champion twice over: he won the 100 Breaststroke in 58.30 and the 200 Individual Medley in 1:54.17, a school record that lowers his own 1:54.90. The boys 200 Medley Relay — Kent Olsson, Wade Olsson, Jackson Eftekhar, Jackson Machamer — won the state title in 1:40.44, a school record 1.36 seconds under the 1:41.80 from 2025. Three of the four — Kent Olsson, Wade Olsson and Jackson Eftekhar — swam on the 2025 record relay; Jackson Machamer is new to the record, in place of Zachary Duerkop. The girls 200 Free Relay took 2nd in 1:45.53.

Freshman Avery Fleming set two Freshman records: 100 Back 1:06.14 (was Sarynn Patterson's 1:06.38 from 2016) and 100 Fly 1:05.75 (was Paisley White's 1:05.90 from 2019). Sophomore Kent Olsson set two Sophomore records: 100 Back 55.27 (was his own 56.52) and 500 Free 4:58.44 (was his own 5:03.13).

18 swims were season bests, led by Quinn Scott (−19.03s, 500 Free), and Rachel Anderson and Kent Olsson are knocking on the door of records.
