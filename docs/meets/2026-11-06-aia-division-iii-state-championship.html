The team story

The girls’ climb

From 8th in 2025, the girls climbed to 5th of 36 teams with 178 points. They finished only 2 points ahead of 6th. Eleven individual swims reached a final.

The boys’ championship tradition

Defending 2025 State champions, the boys finished 4th of 36 teams with 225 points and three event titles. Ten individual swims reached a final.

Relay excellence

The school-record boys 200 Medley Relay (1st at State, 1:40.44); the girls 200 Free Relay (2nd at State, 1:45.53, #5 in team history); and the boys 400 Free Relay (4th at State, 3:22.77, #2 in team history).
