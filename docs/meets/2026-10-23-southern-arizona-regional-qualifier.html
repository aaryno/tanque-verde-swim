📖 The story

Wade Olsson sets the 100 Breaststroke school record

Senior Wade Olsson swam the 100 Breaststroke in 58.11, breaking Zachary Duerkop's 59.51 from Sep 14, 2024. It is also the new Senior record. He also lowered his own 200 Individual Medley record to 1:54.90. Sophomore Kent Olsson set a new Sophomore 100 Backstroke record of 56.52, breaking Nicholas Cusson's 56.59 from 2021.

17 swims were season bests, led by Isla Cerepak (−7.79s, 100 Fly).
